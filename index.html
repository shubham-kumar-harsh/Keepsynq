FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few things people ask most often before getting started.

In this local build, your files are stored in your browser's IndexedDB on the device you use. They never leave your browser — there is no server. Clearing your site data will remove them, so export a backup from Settings regularly.
Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2 (310,000 iterations). The Private Vault — designed for the most sensitive documents — uses AES-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase. In this prototype the vault section is a placeholder; the encryption primitives are already wired.
Yes. The interface is fully responsive, and MAYA, uploads, search and previews all work on mobile. The camera only works when the page is served over HTTPS or localhost — that's a browser security rule, not a Keepsynq limitation.
She tells you. MAYA does not generate fake filenames, fake dates or fake folders. If nothing in your vault matches your question, she returns a clear "I couldn't find a matching document" message.
Open Settings from the dashboard and click "Export backup". You'll download a JSON file containing every file, its original bytes, folders and metadata. You can import it later on the same or another browser.
Not in this build. Everything runs locally. Google sign-in, expiring share links and cloud sync are shown as disabled with a clear reason, rather than faked.
Tesseract.js runs on every uploaded image — screenshots, phone photos, scanned receipts. It extracts the text and stores it with the file, so you can search inside images. PDF OCR is not run in this build.
